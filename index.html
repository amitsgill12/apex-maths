FAQs

Common questions answered

We start with a free 15-minute call to find out about your child's level, exam board and goals. From there we'll recommend the right format and you can get started as soon as the following week.

1-1 sessions are fully personalised to your child's specific gaps and exam board. Group sessions follow a structured curriculum and are ideal for students who need consistent practice.

Sessions run via video call with an interactive whiteboard. Your child needs a device, stable internet, and something to write on. The tutor shares their screen and students show working in real time.

Most students notice improved understanding within the first few sessions. Visible grade improvements typically take a full term. We review progress every half term so you always know where things stand.

Sessions are booked and paid roughly a month in advance — four sessions at a time. No fixed contract. If you decide not to continue, simply don't book the following month.
