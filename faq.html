Getting Started

It's a free 15-minute call — no preparation needed. We'll talk through where your child is right now, what they're finding difficult, and which Locus programme would suit them best. There's no commitment on either side; it's just a conversation to see if we're the right fit.

Yes, completely free. There's no catch and no obligation. If after the call you decide Locus isn't right for your child, that's absolutely fine — the call costs nothing.

If we decide to go ahead, we'll agree on the right programme (1-1, group, or bootcamp), confirm the start date, and you'll receive a confirmation email with all the details. From there, sessions are booked and paid for a month at a time.

How Sessions Work

Sessions take place via video call (Zoom or Google Meet). Your child will need a device with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection, and something to write on — either paper or a tablet. The tutor shares their screen for worked examples, and students can show their working via camera or a shared whiteboard tool.

Pricing & Commitment

1-1 sessions are priced by year group — see our Courses page for the current rates. Group sessions start from £15 per session. Bootcamp weeks are £80 each, booked independently — see our Courses page for dates and topics.

Sessions are booked and paid for roughly a month in advance — approximately four sessions at a time. This gives your child a consistent rhythm and means we can plan a proper sequence of lessons rather than ad-hoc, unconnected sessions.
