About the Owner — Amit

Started at university. Still going.

I've been tutoring maths for over 16 years — starting informally at university and building what eventually became Locus Tuition from the ground up. A maths graduate, I work exclusively with Years 7 to 11 students across GCSE and iGCSE, and have spent that time developing a curriculum that's entirely my own.

Rather than using off-the-shelf resources or generic revision packs, I map each student's syllabus, identify the specific gaps holding them back, and structure every session around what that student actually needs — not what a standard textbook says should come next. The result is tuition that feels nothing like school: focused, logical, and built around the individual.

My specialism is reading what a student needs and adapting in real time. Some need the foundational logic explained before anything else makes sense. Others are technically capable but have one or two gaps quietly costing them marks. I work out which — and teach to it.

The Process

How we transform grades

Every student who joins Locus goes through the same structured process — from that first call to exam day.

Step 01

Free discovery call

A quick 15-minute call to understand where your child is, what they need, and which Locus programme fits best. No commitment.

Step 02

Diagnose the gaps

We identify exactly which topics are holding your child back — right down to the specific syllabus points the exam board will test.

Step 03

Targeted lesson plan

A structured plan built around your child's gaps, exam board, and timeline to exams — not a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

Step 04

Structured sessions

Weekly lessons that teach the system behind the maths — not just methods to memorise, but the logic that makes everything click.

Step 05

Tailored resources

Every session comes with worksheets and practice questions targeting exactly the topics covered — built for your child, not downloaded from the internet.

Step 06

Mock exam practice

Past papers, exam technique, and timed practice built into the programme. No surprises when the real thing arrives.

Step 07

Half-term review

We review progress every half term. You'll see exactly what's improved, what's still being worked on, and what comes next.
